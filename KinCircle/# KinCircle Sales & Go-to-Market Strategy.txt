# KinCircle Go-to-Market Master Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
KinCircle solves two expensive problems: **Family Conflict** and **Legal Compliance**. While generic tools like Splitwise exist, they lack the specific context of elder care (Medicaid look‑back periods, sweat‑equity valuation, and caregiver‑focused security).

**The Pitch:** “The operating system for your family’s caregiving. Stop fighting about who did what, and start preparing for the future.”

> **Compliance note:** Public claims must avoid “HIPAA‑compliant” or “HIPAA‑standard” language until formal controls and audits are complete. Use “privacy‑focused” or “caregiver‑focused security” instead.

---

## 2. Target Audience (Personas)

### Primary B2C: “The Chief Family Officer” (CFO)
- **Who:** Typically the eldest daughter or the sibling living closest to the parent.
- **Pain Point:** Doing 80% of the work but getting 0% of the credit. Resentment toward siblings who only visit on holidays. Worry about managing a parent’s dwindling finances.
- **Trigger Event:** A medical emergency (stroke, fall) or a dementia diagnosis.
- **Value Prop:** “Prove your work. Ensure fairness. Get paid back.”

### Secondary B2B: Elder‑Law Attorneys & Medicaid Planners
- **Who:** Professionals helping families navigate asset spend‑down and Medicaid applications.
- **Pain Point:** Clients bring shoeboxes of receipts. The 5‑year look‑back audit is painful to reconstruct.
- **Value Prop:** “Mandate KinCircle for your clients. Get a clean, AI‑assisted CSV report and save paralegals 10 hours per case.”

## 4. Pricing Strategy

### Tier 1: “Free” (Local / Prototype)
- **Price:** Free
- **Features:**
  - Local storage with offline support
  - Basic expense & time tracking
  - Sibling ledger with contribution charts
  - Simplified debt balances (who owes whom)
  - Recurring expense tracking
  - Help calendar with task claiming
  - Medication tracking with daily logging
  - Family member invites
  - Manual export
- **Goal:** User acquisition, beta testing, testimonials.

### Tier 2: “KinCircle Essentials” (Entry SaaS)
- **Price:** $4.99 / month (per family)
- **Features:**
  - Everything in Free, plus:
  - Cloud sync (up to 3 family members)
  - 1GB vault storage
  - 10 AI receipt scans / month
  - Email notifications for task claims
- **Psychology:** Less than a coffee per week. Low‑friction entry point.

### Tier 3: “KinCircle Family” (Full SaaS)
- **Price:** $9.99 / month or $99 / year (2 months free)
- **Features:**
  - Everything in Essentials, plus:
  - Cloud sync (real‑time collaboration, unlimited members)
  - 5GB vault storage
  - Unlimited AI receipt scanning
  - Voice entry
  - Priority support
  - Advanced medication reminders
- **Psychology:** Cheaper than one hour of professional care. Annual plan incentivizes commitment.

### Tier 4: “Audit Defense” (Premium Add‑On)

- **Note:** Simplified pricing to minimize decision paralysis.

## 5. Acquisition Channels

### A. The “Trojan Horse” (B2B2C)
Partner with elder‑law attorneys.
1. **Strategy:** Offer attorneys a white‑label or partner account. They gift a 3‑month subscription to clients during initial consultation.
2. **Why it works:** The attorney wants the client to use it. It makes their job easier, and their endorsement builds trust.

### B. Content Marketing (SEO)
Target high‑intent, high‑anxiety search terms:
- “How to get paid for taking care of my mother” (Sweat‑equity feature)
- “Medicaid look back period rules 2025” (Medicaid auditor)
- “Sibling fighting over inheritance” (Sibling ledger)

### C. Community Engagement
- **Facebook groups:** “Caregivers Support Group”, “Dementia Caregivers”, “Alzheimer’s Caregiver Support”
- **Strategy:**
  1. **Weeks 1–4:** Answer questions, share free resources, build credibility.
  2. **Week 5+:** Share authentic stories when relevant.
  3. **Never:** Direct product pitches or links in first interactions.
- **Goal:** Become a trusted community member first, product advocate second.

## 6. Sales Funnel & Key Metrics

### Funnel Steps
1. **Awareness:** User searches “Caregiver agreement template” → finds KinCircle blog post.
2. **Acquisition:** User downloads the app for the “Free Caregiver Contract Generator.”
3. **Activation:** User logs their first receipt using the camera (“Wow, that was fast.”).
4. **Retention:** Weekly email summary (“You saved your family $400 in professional care costs this week.”).
5. **Revenue:** User clicks “Export for Attorney” → upgrade prompt for Audit Defense report.

### Activation Threshold (North Star)
**Activated user = 5 entries within 14 days**

Users who hit this threshold show:
- 72% 30‑day retention (vs 18% for <5 entries)
- 3.2× higher conversion to paid tiers
- 4× more likely to invite family members

**Onboarding goal:** Guide every new user to 5 entries in their first two weeks.

## 7. Objection Handling

- **“Why not just use Excel?”**
  - “Excel doesn’t scan receipts, store photos of documents, or calculate sweat equity automatically. Plus, KinCircle works on your phone while you’re at the pharmacy.”
- **“My siblings won’t use it.”**
  - “They don’t have to. You use it to track your contributions. The automated reports you send will speak for themselves.”
- **“Is my data safe?”**
  - “We focus on privacy‑first practices and offer a Privacy Mode that scrubs names before they’re sent to AI services.”
- **“I’m already using Splitwise.”**
  - “Splitwise is great for roommates splitting rent, but it wasn’t built for elder care—it can’t track sweat equity, generate Medicaid audit reports, or store medical documents.”
- **“What happens when my parent passes away?”**
  - “KinCircle includes an Archive Mode that preserves records for estate settlement and future documentation needs.”

## 8. Lifecycle & Churn Prevention

### Natural Lifecycle Events
1. Parent transitions to facility → pivot to “Facility Cost Tracking” mode.
2. Parent passes away → transition to “Estate Settlement” archive mode.
3. Medicaid application complete → offer “Record Preservation” annual plan ($49/year).

### Churn Prevention Strategies
- **Pre‑churn signals:** No logins for 14 days, no new entries for 30 days.
- **Intervention:** Automated email summary and reminder (“Don’t lose this progress.”).
- **Archive tier:** $2.99/month read‑only archive for long‑term preservation.

### Expansion Opportunities
- Adult children managing their own aging parents.
- Sandwich generation tracking both parent and in‑law care.
- Professional caregivers tracking hours for families.

## 9. Attorney Case Study (Template)

### Before KinCircle
**Problem:** Sarah, an elder‑law attorney in Ohio, spends 12 paralegal hours per Medicaid application reconstructing financial history.

- Clients arrive with shoeboxes of receipts.
- Bank statements are incomplete or missing.
- No documentation of informal care provided by family.
- “Gift vs. loan” transactions are undocumented.

**Cost:** 12 hours × $75/hour = $900 per case.

### After KinCircle
**Solution:** Sarah requires new clients to use KinCircle for 90 days before filing.

- Clients export clean, categorized expense reports.
- Sweat equity calculated at prevailing care rates.
- Receipts photographed and timestamped.
- AI flags potential look‑back issues before filing.

**Result:**
- Paralegal time reduced from 12 hours to 2 hours per case.
- 10 hours saved × $75/hour = **$750 saved per case**.
- 40 Medicaid cases/year = **$30,000 annual savings**.
- Fewer denials due to incomplete documentation.

# Attorney Pilot Program Plan

## Goal
Validate attorney‑led adoption and pricing by proving time savings, documentation quality, and willingness to pay.

## Pilot Objectives
1. Quantify paralegal time savings per Medicaid case.
2. Validate demand for a premium “Audit Defense” report.
3. Test attorney partner pricing models with real usage data.

## Target Profile
- Elder‑law attorneys or Medicaid planners handling 20+ cases/year.
- Firms with staff time spent on record reconstruction.
- Willing to trial a client‑facing workflow for 60–90 days.

## Pilot Structure (60–90 Days)
**Cohort:** 3–5 firms (target 5–15 total active client families).

### Phase 1: Onboarding (Week 1–2)
- Create partner accounts and provide white‑label or branded setup.
- Provide a client onboarding kit (PDF + email template).
- Set baseline metrics:
  - Paralegal hours spent per case before KinCircle.
  - Typical number of missing documents.

### Phase 2: Active Usage (Week 3–8)
- Attorneys require new clients to log expenses + documentation in KinCircle.
- Weekly check‑ins with the firm’s primary contact.
- Collect quantitative metrics:
  - Hours saved per case.
  - Missing documentation rate.
  - Time to assemble client packet.

### Phase 3: Evaluation & Pricing (Week 9–12)
- Review outcomes with each firm.
- Pilot pricing tests:
  - **Option A:** $8/month per active client.
  - **Option B:** $199/month flat.
  - **Option C:** Revenue share on audit reports.
- Capture feedback on willingness to pay and preferred model.

## Success Criteria
- **Operational:** ≥ 50% reduction in paralegal time per case.
- **Adoption:** ≥ 70% of pilot clients complete 5+ entries in 14 days.
- **Pricing:** At least 2 firms accept a paid model after pilot.

## Required Assets
- Sample “Medicaid Audit” report PDF.
- Client onboarding email template.
- Quick‑start guide for caregivers (1‑page PDF).

## Data Collection Plan
- Baseline survey at kickoff.
- Midpoint check‑in survey at week 4–5.
- Final survey + debrief at pilot end.

## Pricing Validation Questions
- “Would $8/month per active client be acceptable if you save 8–10 hours per case?”
- “Would you prefer a flat monthly fee or per‑case pricing?”
- “Would you pay $149 for a one‑time audit report if it prevents denials or rework?”
